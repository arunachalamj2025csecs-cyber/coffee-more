# MASTER WEBSITE DESIGN PROMPT – AR COFFEE SHOP

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a senior UI/UX designer, professional website content writer, brand designer and full-stack web application architect.

Design and develop a complete, modern, premium coffee shop website for a brand named **AR Coffee Shop**.

The website must have two completely separate but interconnected portals:

1. Customer Portal – For customers to explore the coffee shop, browse products, place orders, track orders, access offers and submit reviews.
2. Owner Portal – For the shop owner to manage products, categories, orders, customers, offers, reviews, sales analytics and business settings.

Both portals must share the same backend and database.

The website should look like a premium specialty coffee brand rather than a generic restaurant ordering template.

## 2. BUSINESS INFORMATION

- Business Name: AR Coffee Shop
- Business Model: Single physical coffee shop
- Currency: USD ($)
- Business Type: Coffee Shop / Café
- Design Style: Modern, Premium, Minimalist and Elegant
- Ordering Methods: Dine-in and Takeaway / Pickup
- Payment Methods: UPI, Credit/Debit Cards and Cash
- Customer Authentication: Email and Password
- Owner Authentication: Secure Owner Login
- Menu Management: Fully Dynamic
- Customer Order Tracking: Enabled
- Offers and Discounts: Enabled
- Customer Reviews and Ratings: Enabled

Important:
The owner must be able to add, edit, delete, hide, update prices and manage the availability of all menu items through the Owner Portal. Never hardcode the menu into the customer interface.

Currency and payment processing must be configured separately. UPI is generally an INR-based payment method, so ensure that the payment gateway supports the actual transaction currency. Do not assume UPI supports USD settlement.

# PART D – FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

## 34. CUSTOMER AND OWNER INTEGRATION

Both portals must share a common backend and database.

### Dynamic Menu Workflow

1. Owner signs into the Owner Portal.
2. Owner opens Product Management.
3. Owner adds a product called Hazelnut Latte.
4. Owner uploads an image, adds a description and sets a price.
5. Owner publishes the product.
6. Product information is saved in the database.
7. Customer Portal fetches the updated menu.
8. Customers can now see and order Hazelnut Latte.

### Order Management Workflow

1. Customer selects products.
2. Customer adds products to the cart.
3. Customer chooses Dine-in or Takeaway.
4. Customer confirms the order.
5. Order is saved in the database.
6. Owner receives a new order notification.
7. Owner accepts and prepares the order.
8. Owner updates the order status.
9. Customer sees the latest status on the tracking page.

### Offer Management Workflow

1. Owner creates a new promotion.
2. Owner configures its discount and validity.
3. Offer is saved in the database.
4. Customer Portal displays the active offer.
5. Customer applies a valid coupon.
6. Backend validates the coupon and recalculates the order total.

### Review Workflow

1. Customer completes an eligible order.
2. Customer submits a rating and review.
3. Review is saved in the database.
4. Owner views the review.
5. Owner can respond or moderate inappropriate content.
6. Approved reviews appear on the customer website.
